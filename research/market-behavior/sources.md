# Source Ledger

Access and review date for every source: 2026-07-31.

## Source Policy

The [paper manifest](papers/manifest.md) is the authoritative source index. It
records full-text status, findings, limitations, and the permitted agent use for
eleven primary papers. Publisher records, NBER copies, and author or university
copies were preferred. Confidence is high that the manifest represents the
authors' reported results; confidence is lower when transferring any result to
a different participant group, asset, venue, regime, or forecast horizon.

## Interpretation Policy

- Behavioral constructs are candidate mechanisms, not observed facts.
- Observable market variables are proxies and may have nonbehavioral causes.
- Correlation, a calibrated theoretical model, and a laboratory choice result
  have different evidentiary strength and must not be blended.
- Historical sample results require current corroboration before affecting a
  live probability.
- Counterevidence is decision-relevant. The herding study is included to prevent
  the agent from generalizing a crowd story across institutional participants.

## Repository Use

The durable research base informs skill controls. Live symbol research must use
current online evidence and cite it near the claim; it must not cite this dossier
as proof that today's market is exhibiting a specific behavior.
