# Financial Analyst Agent Research

## Problem Summary

The repository began as a C++23/TBB development-agent template. This research
defines an evidence-based replacement: a financial analyst and broker-support
agent that can investigate companies and markets, value securities, evaluate
news, reason about macroeconomics, manage portfolio and execution risk, and
continue improving through tested repository changes.

## Scope

- Review primary research on trading and market microstructure, company
  analysis, economic foundations, and financial-news research.
- Identify professional standards for investment research, client-facing
  recommendations, broker conduct, conflicts, and execution quality.
- Decide which existing skills to retain or remove and which finance skills to
  add without duplicating responsibility.
- Define verification for both financial outputs and future agent changes.

## Non-Goals

- Claiming that the agent is a licensed broker, investment adviser, fiduciary,
  or compliance professional.
- Promising returns or converting historical anomalies directly into trades.
- Connecting to a brokerage or granting autonomous order-routing authority.
- Selecting a single jurisdiction's rules as universally applicable.

## Research Status

Complete as of 2026-07-31. Forty-one primary papers were reviewed in full:
10 trading papers, 10 company-analysis papers, 10 economics papers, and 11
financial-news papers. Current primary professional and regulatory sources were
also reviewed.
