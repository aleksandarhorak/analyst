# Source Ledger

Access date for every source: 2026-07-31.

## Source Policy

Primary research, official regulators, central banks, standards bodies, and
publisher or author copies were preferred. The [paper manifest](papers/manifest.md)
is the authoritative paper-level source index and records title, authors, year,
venue, URL, full-text status, findings, limitations, and implementation impact
for all 41 papers. Confidence is high for bibliographic metadata and for claims
reported as the authors' findings; confidence is lower when generalizing an old,
single-market, observational, or stylized result to current live trading.

## Professional Research Standards

- **CFA Institute, Standard V(A): Diligence and Reasonable Basis.**
  https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-v-a
  Updated April 2024. Requires diligence, independence, thoroughness, and a
  reasonable research basis. Confidence: high.
- **CFA Institute, Standard V(B): Communication with Clients and Prospective
  Clients.**
  https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-v-b
  Updated January 2024. Requires material methods, risks, limitations, and clear
  separation of facts from opinions. Confidence: high.
- **CFA Institute, Standard I(B): Independence and Objectivity.**
  https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-i-b
  Updated April 2024. Supports conflict disclosure, independent judgment, and
  multi-source due diligence. Confidence: high.
- **CFA Institute, Standard III(D): Performance Presentation.**
  https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-iii-d
  Updated April 2024. Requires fair, accurate, complete performance reporting
  and disclosure of simulated results. Confidence: high.

## Broker, Suitability, And Execution Standards

- **SEC Regulation Best Interest.**
  https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/regulation-best-interest
  Requires disclosure, care, conflict-of-interest, and compliance obligations;
  costs and reasonably available alternatives are material. Confidence: high
  for U.S. broker-dealers in covered circumstances.
- **SEC Staff Bulletin: Standards of Conduct Care Obligations.**
  https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/broker-dealers/staff-bulletin-standards-conduct-broker-dealers-investment-advisers-care-obligations
  Explains product understanding, customer profile, alternatives, costs, and
  series-of-transactions review. Confidence: high as staff guidance; it is not
  itself a rule.
- **FINRA Rule 2090: Know Your Customer.**
  https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2090
  Requires reasonable diligence to know and retain essential customer facts.
  Confidence: high for FINRA members.
- **FINRA Rule 2111: Suitability.**
  https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2111
  Defines customer-profile factors and guards against unsuitable or excessive
  recommendations. Confidence: high for applicable U.S. activity.
- **FINRA Rule 2241: Research Analysts and Research Reports.**
  https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2241
  Requires policies for objective research and management/disclosure of analyst,
  banking, and trading conflicts. Confidence: high for FINRA members.
- **FINRA Rule 5310: Best Execution and Interpositioning.**
  https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5310
  Requires reasonable diligence and regular review for favorable customer terms;
  inducements cannot replace best execution. Confidence: high.
- **FCA COBS 2.1: Client's Best Interests Rule.**
  https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs2
  Requires covered firms to act honestly, fairly, professionally, and in client
  interests. Confidence: high for applicable UK activity.
- **FCA COBS 9.2 and 9.3: Suitability.**
  https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/COBS/9/2.html
  https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs9/cobs9s3
  Requires necessary client information and warns that individually plausible
  trades can be collectively unsuitable or excessive. Confidence: high.
- **ESMA MiFID II Suitability Guidelines.**
  https://www.esma.europa.eu/document/guidelines-certain-aspects-mifid-ii-suitability-requirements-1
  Provides current EU suitability guidance. Confidence: high for covered EU
  firms; implementation remains jurisdiction- and role-specific.

## Additional Controls

- **SEC Investment Adviser Marketing Rule guide.**
  https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing
  Supports fair presentation, net-versus-gross consistency, and controls for
  hypothetical performance. Confidence: high for covered U.S. advisers.
- **SEC Investor Alert: Social Media and Investing Fraud.**
  https://www.sec.gov/investor/alerts/socialmediaandfraud.pdf
  Supports explicit manipulation, impersonation, and pump-and-dump checks for
  social-media claims. Confidence: high as investor-protection guidance.

## Interpretation Boundary

Rules change and depend on jurisdiction, legal role, client type, product, and
facts. The agent must retrieve current primary law or regulation for live advice,
state the assumed capacity, and escalate legal or compliance conclusions. These
sources define conservative controls; they do not authorize the agent to perform
regulated activity.
