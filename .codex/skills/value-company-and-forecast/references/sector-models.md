# Sector Forecast And Valuation Branches

Select the economically relevant branch; do not force every company into a
generic revenue-margin-DCF template. Conglomerates may need several branches and
a sum of parts. Reconcile every sector metric to audited statements and explain
definitions that differ across issuers.

## Banks And Lenders

- Drivers: earning assets, deposit mix/beta, loan and securities yields, funding
  costs, net interest income/margin, fees, expenses, charge-offs, delinquencies,
  provisions and allowance coverage.
- Balance/risk: duration and accumulated other comprehensive income, uninsured
  and concentrated funding, liquidity, CET1/RWA, stress losses, off-balance-sheet
  exposures, loan vintages, and regulatory constraints.
- Valuation: tangible book and normalized return on tangible equity, residual
  income/dividend capacity, and peer P/TBV or P/E with credit/capital differences.
  Avoid enterprise-value/EBITDA and generic FCFF as primary bank methods.

## Insurers

- Drivers: policies/exposure, pricing, retention, premiums, loss and expense
  ratios, reserve development, catastrophe load, reinsurance, investment yield,
  duration, and capital generation.
- Balance/risk: reserve adequacy, asset-liability matching, credit risk,
  guarantees/options, reinsurance recoverables, solvency/RBC, and catastrophe
  aggregation.
- Valuation: normalized book value/ROE, residual income, dividend capacity,
  embedded value where appropriate, and segment sum of parts. Treat float as a
  liability whose economic cost depends on underwriting quality.

## Semiconductors And Hardware

- Drivers: end-market units, content, ASP, mix, node/product transitions,
  utilization, yield, wafer/input cost, inventory/channel correction, lead times,
  design wins, backlog quality, and customer/supplier concentration.
- Structure: fabless/foundry/IDM economics, capital intensity, depreciation,
  subsidies, packaging/memory cycles, export controls, and obsolescence.
- Valuation: through-cycle margins/earnings and FCF, DCF with explicit capex and
  cycle, peer multiples normalized for mix/cycle, and asset/foundry separation
  where useful. Do not annualize a peak or trough quarter without a cycle bridge.

## SaaS And Software

- Drivers: ARR/revenue cohorts, new bookings, RPO, gross retention, net revenue
  retention, seats/usage, pricing, churn, gross margin, sales efficiency,
  payback, R&D, support and cloud cost.
- Quality: contract duration, implementation/services mix, capitalization,
  billings/cash timing, customer concentration, usage elasticity, stock-based
  compensation, dilution, and acquisition dependency.
- Valuation: DCF and reverse DCF, EV/revenue or EV/gross profit only with growth,
  margin, retention and reinvestment normalization, plus per-share dilution.
  Rule-of-40-style summaries are diagnostics, not standalone values.

## Energy And Natural Resources

- Drivers: production/throughput, realized benchmark and differential, hedge
  book, decline curves, reserves/resources, lifting/processing cost, royalties,
  transport, maintenance/growth capex, working capital, taxes and decommissioning.
- Risk: reserve audit and life, project sanction/ramp, fiscal terms, outages,
  environmental liabilities, midstream commitments, commodity basis/FX, and
  balance-sheet survival at stress prices.
- Valuation: asset/project NAV with price/cost scenarios and discount timing,
  mid-cycle DCF/FCF, reserve and transaction metrics, and segment sum of parts.
  Reconcile equity value to debt, leases, reclamation and minority interests.

## Cyclicals, Industrials, Autos, And Consumer Discretionary

- Drivers: units, price/mix, orders, backlog conversion/cancellation, capacity
  and utilization, labor/material/freight, dealer/channel inventory, incentives,
  warranty, working capital, maintenance/growth capex and replacement cycles.
- Regime: distinguish structural share/margin change from cyclical operating
  leverage; test recession, rates, credit availability and input/supply shocks.
- Valuation: mid-cycle earnings/FCF and normalized multiples, DCF across a full
  cycle, asset/replacement value or sum of parts where relevant. Show peak-to-
  trough liquidity and dilution before assigning upside to recovery.

## Required Cross-Checks

- Reconcile sector KPIs to financial statements and source definitions.
- Use base/bull/bear assumptions that move together economically.
- Reverse-engineer current price expectations and compare with a simple
  historical/peer baseline.
- Preserve capital, dilution, pension, lease, tax, currency, regulatory and
  off-balance-sheet claims.
- Register published directional probabilities with
  `calibrate-financial-forecasts`; retain model/method version and evidence IDs.
