### Product Checks

- Precious metals: troy-ounce convention, real yields/USD, lease/forward rates,
  official/ETF/central-bank/physical flows, mine supply and recycling.
- Crude: grade and location, barrels, stocks by hub, refinery runs, outages,
  OPEC+/non-OPEC supply, freight, storage and delivery constraints.
- Coffee: grade and deliverable origins, pounds, crop years, certified stocks,
  flowering/harvest weather, disease, freight, BRL and producer behavior.
- Equity-index futures: cash-index methodology, dividends/rates/fair value,
  contract multiplier, expiry/roll, settlement and overnight margin/liquidity.
