# Analyze News Catalysts

## Procedure

1. Resolve the entity, security, event type, jurisdiction, market session, and
   analysis cutoff. Guard against namesakes and stale recirculated stories.
2. Find the originating filing, release, court record, regulator, transcript,
   official statement, or direct data. Treat coverage and social posts as leads.
3. Reconstruct event time, first publication, corrections, revisions,
   embargoes, leaks, and price/volume chronology.
4. Extract actors, action, object, quantities, effective dates, conditions, and
   source confidence before classifying tone.
5. Compare the verified event with prior expectations and current price. State
   what is genuinely new.
6. Map first- and second-order effects through cash flows, financing, discount
   rates, counterparties, supply chains, policy, and positioning.
7. Separate a temporary attention or flow effect from a durable change in
   fundamentals. Test materiality across scenarios.
8. Check provenance, incentives, coordinated amplification, synthetic content,
   manipulation risk, and independent corroboration.
9. State what is priced in only as a supported inference, with alternatives and
   disconfirmers.

Use [the catalyst template](references/catalyst-analysis.md).

## Rules

- Generic positive/negative sentiment is not economic analysis.
- A fast-spreading or novel claim is not necessarily true.
- Do not trade or recommend trading on suspected material non-public
  information; stop and escalate.
- Preserve corrections and contradictions in the chronology.

## Output

Provide verified facts, chronology, prior expectation, surprise, materiality,
transmission map, observed market response, alternative explanations,
misinformation or manipulation flags, scenarios, confidence, and follow-ups.
