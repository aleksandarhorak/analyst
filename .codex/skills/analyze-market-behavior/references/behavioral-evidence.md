# Behavioral Evidence Guide

Use this guide to constrain live analysis. The full review and source links are
in `research/market-behavior/` at the repository root.

## Mechanism Map

| Mechanism | Potential observation | Permitted inference | Required caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reference dependence / disposition | Ownership cost basis or prior high-volume price zones; realized sell behavior | Supply may change around a supported reference point | A chart level alone does not reveal investors' reference points |
| Limited attention | Abnormal news reach, searches, volume, or extreme return | A security may enter more investors' consideration set | Attention does not determine buy/sell direction or value |
| Slow information diffusion | Novel verified information, low coverage, gradual response | Underreaction is a candidate short-horizon path | Mechanical execution or delayed source access may explain the path |
| Feedback / extrapolation | Trend-following flows, survey beliefs, continuing volume | Continuation and later reversal are scenarios | Price momentum alone is circular evidence |
| Sentiment and limits to arbitrage | Direct sentiment measure plus hard-to-value, constrained security | Temporary relative mispricing is plausible | Proxy construction, risk, borrow, liquidity, and regime matter |
| Self-attribution / overconfidence | Success followed by increased turnover or leverage | Aggressive trading may outpace information quality | Volume has many causes; private confidence is unobserved |
| Media language | Verified facts separated from tone, reach, timing, and response | Tone-related pressure or reversal can be tested | One article or word score is not a causal signal |
| Herding / social proof | Participant-specific correlated flows or holdings | Coordinated direction may reinforce a move | Historical institutional evidence is mixed, especially in large stocks |
| Capitulation / panic | Forced flows, volatility, depth loss, margin stress, gap and recovery | Liquidation pressure may create path dependence | A large decline alone is insufficient |

## Evidence Grades

- **Direct:** participant-specific flows, holdings, surveys, or experimentally
  elicited beliefs with correct timing.
- **Strong proxy:** multiple independent market observations aligned with a
  mechanism and no dominant mechanical explanation.
- **Weak proxy:** price, generic volume, broad tone, or a chart level without
  participant identification.
- **Narrative only:** an emotion label or crowd claim unsupported by an
  observation. Do not use it.

## Source Base

The repository review covers Kahneman and Tversky (reference dependence), Hong
and Stein (information diffusion and feedback), Barber and Odean (attention),
Baker and Wurgler (sentiment), Gervais and Odean (overconfidence), Odean
(disposition and excessive trading), Greenwood and Shleifer (extrapolative
expectations), Tetlock (media language), Shiller (excess-volatility benchmark),
and Lakonishok, Shleifer, and Vishny (institutional-herding counterevidence).
Use the original papers and current observations when the conclusion depends on
a mechanism.
